## 🚨 UPDATE (2026-09-07): "CBH Person-First Lead Form v1" was DELIVERING Medicaid/Medicare leads — root cause = "Submit the form" vs "Close the form" (FIXED)
The person-first form (built ~9/02, person-first order = who→struggling→insurance→provider) **fixed conversion (leads flowing again after the 0-lead clinician run) BUT was leaking disqualified leads.** Lead export literally showed "How do you get your health insurance? = Medicaid or state plan / Medicare" on ~12 of the delivered leads. Not mis-selection — the gate genuinely wasn't blocking.
- **★ ROOT CAUSE (the gotcha to remember):** the Medicaid/Medicare answers routed to the E2 non-lead page **via the "Submit the form" action, not "Close the form."** Meta's per-answer routing has 3 actions: *Go to a question* / **Submit the form** ("give you their info + see a custom end page **for leads**") / **Close the form** ("**don't** give you their info + end page **for non-leads**"). **Only "Close the form" prevents a lead.** "Submit the form → [nice 'we can't accept Medicaid' page]" STILL captures + delivers the lead — the disqualifying end-page content is cosmetic. This is why the on-screen routing *looked* correct (pointed at the E2 page) yet leaked. **RULE: every disqualifying answer must use the "Close the form" action, and land on a NON-LEAD end page.**
- **★ FIX IMPLEMENTED 9/07 — via a NEW form in a DUPLICATED campaign (published forms can't be edited, so no in-place fix was possible):** couldn't edit the leaking form, so **duplicated the whole campaign and built a NEW form identical to v1 EXCEPT the routing.** New routing: Q3 "How do you get your health insurance?" → Medicaid or state plan = **Close the form → End page for non-leads**; Medicare = **Close the form → non-leads**; **Not sure = Go to Q4** (proceed/keep — genuine "don't know my plan" prospects, verify on call); Employer/Spouse/Marketplace → Q4 → Submit → lead end page. **OPEN:** (a) verify the non-lead end page has the real SAMHSA content (1-800-662-4357 / findtreatment.gov), not a blank page that "Close the form" can spawn; (b) PUBLISH the new form → **mandatory LIVE Medicaid submission test (confirm NO lead delivered)**; (c) attach the new form to the ads + **send the new form_id to the dev for LeadsBridge remap** ("Colorado Behavioral Health CRM Connection"); (d) manually disqualify the ~12 Medicaid/Medicare leads already collected on the old form; (e) pause the old leaking campaign to avoid audience overlap.
- **★ AUDIT [[wavecrest-detox-ads]] forms for the same bug** — if WC's "blocks Medicaid" form used "Submit the form → page" instead of "Close the form," WC has been silently delivering (and paying for) unqualified Medicaid/Medicare leads too, inflating its reported CPL. Pull a WC lead export and check the insurance column.
- Implication: this form's earlier "leads flowing" success was **partly inflated by unqualified leads** — true qualified-lead rate is lower; re-judge after the gate actually fires.
- **PROGRESS (later 9/07): new "CBH Person-First Lead Form v1" now SWITCHED onto all 5 ads** in the duplicated campaign (advances open item (c) — form attached; still TODO: publish/live Medicaid test, send form_id to dev for LeadsBridge remap, disqualify the ~12 old Medicaid leads, pause old leaking campaign).
- **★ TRAP CAUGHT — "Related Media" auto-adds a 2nd creative:** on one of the 5 ads the form-attach preview showed TWO creatives side-by-side ("Real support for the hardest days" + "A clinician texts back in 10 minutes") = Meta had auto-loaded a **Related Media** suggestion as a second asset in the single ad, silently corrupting the one-creative-per-ad-set A/B (Meta would rotate/optimize between them, blending reporting). **Tell: the preview shows an "All" thumbnail strip + two full ad cards instead of one card you cycle through placements.** FIX = edit ad → delete the Related Media asset → back to one creative. User confirmed this was the cause + deleted it. Also always re-verify after any media edit: enhancements 0/x, multi-advertiser OFF, and the FORM stays attached (media edits can drop the form).
- **★ FORM-VALIDATION insight (told user):** Meta saving/publishing a form only proves STRUCTURAL validity (privacy URL present, required fields filled, completion action set, form attached) — it does NOT catch LOGICAL breakage (wrong form attached, gate not actually blocking [see the Submit-vs-Close bug above], wrong completion phone, wrong privacy page, lead retrieval unwired). "It saved" ≠ "it's correct." Only a REAL live test submission through the chain proves logic. → reinforces the mandatory live Medicaid-submission test before trusting the new gate.
