**SameDayPsych** — telepsychiatry brand, subsidiary of Wavecrest (behavioral health). Positioning: same-day psychiatric access. User created their FB Page (owns it) and runs hiring ads from their own ad account (billing user's card; beneficiary = SameDayPsych). Part of [[meta-ads-client-work]].

**Use case:** Meta ads to **recruit remote PMHNPs and psychiatrists**. Because it's a hiring ad, it MUST use the **Employment** special ad category → no age/gender targeting, 15-mi min radius, profession/health interests removed. Strategy: broad geo + strong "Licensed PMHNP or Psychiatrist?" copy hook + retarget site visitors; LinkedIn recommended in parallel for job-title targeting.

**Landing page:** `provider.samedaypsych.com` = the full **provider credentialing application** (asks for medical license, DEA, CV, malpractice, e-signature). This is HIGH friction for cold ad traffic — recommended putting a lightweight Meta Instant Lead Form (name/email/license type/state) in FRONT of it, with the Lead Form Completion screen linking to the full application. Meta doesn't email leads natively — need Zapier/CRM for auto follow-up.

**Business verification** for their ad account was submitted (was pending as of ~mid-2026). Ad-transparency beneficiary/advertiser verification is a separate step from business verification.

Ads were drafted with 5 creative angles (autonomy/flexibility, mission/access "same-day", compensation, burnout/work-life, Reels short-form). Full mockups delivered in-chat.
