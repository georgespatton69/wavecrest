- **Two-phase pricing:** Phase 1 Investigation = flat **$495–$895** (pull IRS transcripts, diagnose case); fee credits toward Phase 2. Phase 2 Resolution = quoted per file after review.
- **Target customer:** taxpayers with **$10,000+** in IRS/state tax debt facing active collections — wage garnishments, bank levies, federal tax liens. Explicitly **declines cases under $10K** (sends them to DIY IRS payment plans). This is a HIGH-INTENT, high-ticket lead — the qualifier (debt amount) is the whole game.

Credibility: **Licensed Enrolled Agent (EA)**, CPA-led, BBB A+, claims **$220M+ resolved for ~5,000 clients**, "Top 10 2025."
- **No guarantees** (compliance-aware): explicitly states it does NOT guarantee any specific reduction; eligibility is the IRS's call.
